Privacy Policy

Ads Ops AI · Uppercuz Creative Co., Ltd. ("UPPERCUZ", "we") · Last updated: 20 October 2026

Ads Ops AI is an internal tool used only by authorized UPPERCUZ team members to manage the advertising accounts of our clients. This policy explains what data the tool accesses and how we protect it.

1. Data we access

  • Advertising account data from Google Ads, Meta (Facebook / Instagram) Ads and TikTok Ads, only for accounts whose owners have granted UPPERCUZ access: account and campaign settings, ads and creatives, and performance metrics such as spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, revenue and audience breakdowns (age, gender, placement, region).
  • Sign-in information of our own team members (name and work email) needed to authorize access through Google, Meta or TikTok sign-in.

We do not collect personal data of the people who see the ads. Audience data is only received in aggregated form from the advertising platforms.

2. How we use the data

  • To produce reports and alerts for the client who owns the advertising account.
  • To help our team decide on budgets, audiences and creatives for that client.
  • In a later phase, to make campaign changes that a team member has approved.

Data from Google APIs is used in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use the data for advertising to anyone, and we do not sell it.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share the data with third parties. A client's data is shown only to that client and to the UPPERCUZ team members working on their account. We may disclose data if required by law.

4. Storage and security

  • Data is stored on systems controlled by UPPERCUZ. Access tokens are kept server-side, are never shared and are not shown in the user interface.
  • Access is limited to authorized team members.
  • We request read-only access unless a feature needs more, and every write action is approved by a person and logged.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep performance data while we manage the client's account. When a client or user revokes access, or asks us to delete their data, we delete it within 30 days. You can also revoke access at any time in your Google Account (myaccount.google.com/permissions), Meta Business settings or TikTok Business Center.

6. Your rights

Under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) you may ask to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you. Contact us at the address below.
