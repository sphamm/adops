Ads Ops AI

An internal advertising operations tool of Uppercuz Creative Co., Ltd. (UPPERCUZ), a digital advertising agency in Bangkok, Thailand.

Ads Ops AI is used only by the UPPERCUZ media team to manage the advertising accounts of clients who have authorized UPPERCUZ to run their ads. It is not offered to the public and there is no public sign-up.

What it does

  • Reads advertising performance (spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, revenue) from Google Ads, Meta Ads and TikTok Ads for client accounts linked to UPPERCUZ.
  • Shows daily reports and fixed-rule alerts so our team can see where budget should move.
  • In a later phase, lets our team adjust campaigns; every change needs approval by a team member and is logged.
